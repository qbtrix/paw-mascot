The code in this repository is MIT (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This file covers
the art.

## What this covers

- **The Paw character**: the glass puppy — head, ears, eyes, glow — as drawn
  in the engine's `PAW_ART` paths
  (`web/_fx/effects/paw-avatar/index.js`) and in every render of it here:
  the app icons, the tray icon, the favicon, the site's social image.
- **The nine friends** on the site: the flat heads in `site/friends/`
  (cat, fox, bunny, bear, panda, axolotl, penguin, capybara, dragon).
