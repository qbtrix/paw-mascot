hub

Every session, one pet.

One pet, however many sessions you run. Hover it and every session is listed beside it: answer one with a quick chat right there, or jump into Claude Code or Codex. Click the pet itself and it shakes awake to listen.

control

Hover the pet, act on its session.

Focus, restart or stop a session from the pet that watches it. The gear opens the pet's settings: switch to another pet, tune its personality, see what it remembers, and glance at your week. It all lives in a .soul file (Soul Protocol, an open format) on your Mac. Memory comes after Pro's first release.
